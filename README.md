## About Me

I am Ashish Mahnwa, a builder in motion from Delhi, India. I move between product, business, fitness, and leadership with one simple instinct: learn deeply, build honestly, and keep improving what I touch.

I am the founder of Atom OS, a gym management SaaS shaped by real operator pain points and a belief that useful software should make ambitious businesses easier to run. Along the way, I work as a product designer, full stack developer, student entrepreneur, certified fitness trainer, certified nutritionist, and someone who still treats every new challenge like a field to explore.

My work sits at the intersection of systems and people. I care about products that scale, teams that move with clarity, and a life built with discipline, curiosity, and momentum.
